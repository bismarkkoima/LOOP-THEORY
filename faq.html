Questions, answered

The ones we are actually asked, in the order we are asked them. If yours is not here, reply to any order email and a person will answer it.

Sizing

How do I know my ring size?

Measure a ring you already wear rather than your finger — wrap a strip of paper around the band's inside edge and match the length against a printable size chart. Fingers change through the day and through the year; a ring that already fits does not.

Every ring lists its range on the product page. Some are cut to a specific size, some are adjustable, and the listing says which.

What if it arrives the wrong size?

One free resize within 60 days. After that any local jeweller can do it, usually faster than posting it back to us. Full detail on Shipping & Returns.

How long are the chains?

Necklaces run 16" with a 2" extender, 18" or 20", listed per piece. Bracelets are 6.5" with an extender, 7", or one size for bangles and cuffs. Earrings list their diameter or drop.

Materials

What are the pieces actually made of?

Five finishes across the collection: 14k gold vermeil over sterling silver, brushed brass, patina bronze, polished steel, and a two-tone brass-and-bronze mix. Each product page names its own.

Will it turn my skin green?

Brass and bronze can, on some people, in humidity. It is a harmless reaction between skin acidity and copper, it washes off, and it is more likely in summer. Steel and vermeil will not.

Is anything nickel-free?

The steel and the vermeil are safe for most sensitive ears. Brass and bronze contain trace nickel. If you react to costume jewelry, stay with those two.

Care

Can I shower or swim in it?

Steel, yes. Everything else, no — chlorine and salt are hard on plating, and soap builds a film under stones and inside chain links that dulls the finish.

Last thing on, first thing off is the rule that keeps jewelry looking new.

The brass has gone dark. Is it faulty?

No — that is the point of it. Brass and bronze darken where they are handled and stay bright where they are not, which is why a worn piece stops looking like a new one. A dry cloth brings it back if you would rather it stayed bright.

Vermeil is different: it is a plating, and it does eventually wear through at the points of most contact. That is wear, not a defect, and it is why we do not put vermeil on the inside of a ring band.

How should I store it?

Dry, dark, and not touching other pieces. The box it arrives in is fine. Chains keep best done up rather than loose.

Orders

Why does it take two to four weeks?

Because nothing is sitting in a warehouse. Pieces are made by our maker network after you order, in the size you asked for. It is the same reason the prices are what they are.

Can I have something engraved?

Signet 01 and Pinky Signet ship with a blank face, ready for a monogram at any local jeweller — they will do a better job than a mail-order engraving, and you can see the work before it is cut. The ID Bar Bracelet takes an engraving cleanly on its underside.

Note that engraved pieces cannot be returned.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until it goes to the maker — usually a day or two. Reply to your order email. After that, the 30-day return window covers you.

Do you restock sold-out pieces?

Usually. Small-batch means a run ends rather than a shelf empties, and a piece that people keep asking for comes back. Some do not, and we would rather say so than keep a listing up forever.
