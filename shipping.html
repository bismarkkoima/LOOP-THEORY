Shipping & returns

When it ships

Most pieces leave our makers two to four weeks after you order. Nothing is warehoused. A ring is cut, finished and hand-checked once you have asked for it, in the size you asked for.

You will get one email when the order is received and another when it is handed to the carrier, with tracking. If a piece is running late you will hear from us before the four weeks are up, not after.

Rings take the longest

Anything sized — rings above all — sits at the slower end of that window. Adjustable pieces, chains and hoops usually move faster.

What it costs

  • Free on orders over $150, sent tracked.
  • $8 flat below $150, also tracked.
  • $22 outside the contiguous US, where we can ship at all.

Duties and import charges are not included and are not something we can calculate for you. If your country charges them, they are yours to pay on delivery.

Returns

Thirty days from delivery, for any reason or none. The piece needs to come back unworn and in its box — a bracelet that has been worn to dinner is usually obvious, and we would rather say that plainly than argue about it later.

Start a return by replying to your order email. You will get a prepaid label if the piece was faulty or we sent the wrong thing, and a $6 label deduction otherwise. Refunds go back to the original payment method within five working days of arrival.

What cannot come back

  • Engraved or otherwise personalised pieces, once the engraving is done.
  • Earrings, if the seal on the box has been broken — a hygiene rule, not ours.
  • Anything altered by a jeweller who is not one of ours.

Exchanges and resizing

There is no exchange process, because holding stock is the thing we do not do. Send the piece back and order the size you want — that is faster than routing it through us twice.

One free resize within 60 days on any ring bought in the wrong size. After that, any competent local jeweller can do it more quickly than we can, and we would rather you kept wearing it.

If something arrives wrong

Damaged, faulty, or simply not what the listing said: tell us within 30 days and it is our problem, not yours. Prepaid label, replacement or full refund, your choice. A photograph of the piece and the box speeds this up considerably.

Plating wears. Patina moves. Neither is a fault — the FAQ goes into what each finish does over time, so you can tell the difference between a piece ageing and a piece failing.
